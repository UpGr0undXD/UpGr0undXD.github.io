Representative work

Piano Concerto No.3

(op.7 2026)
这首第三钢协规模宏大，变化多端，沉重而复杂。动机在挣扎中发展，旋律在不断递进。全曲不分乐章，连续演奏。
参考了肖斯塔科维奇第五交响曲。
It is a large-scale, complex, and heavy work. The motifs develop in struggle, and the melodies progress continuously. The entire piece is performed without breaks between movements.
Inspired by Shostakovich's Symphony No. 5.
